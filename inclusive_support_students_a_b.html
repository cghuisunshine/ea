Inclusive Supports in Education

Adaptations in the Classroom

Selected supports and reasons based on the profiles of Students A and B.

A

Terry - Grade 5

Profile: Terry works at Grade 5 level in all core subjects except reading. Decoding is difficult, but his comprehension is strong.

1 Access

Audiobooks or text-to-speech

This allows Terry to access Grade 5 content without decoding difficulties limiting his understanding.

2 Time

Extra time for reading tasks

Terry may need additional time to decode words accurately and complete reading activities successfully.

3 Instruction

Direct phonics and decoding support

Targeted instruction addresses his specific reading need while keeping other learning at Grade 5 level.

✓ Main goal: Remove the decoding barrier while maintaining Grade 5 learning expectations.
B

Josh - Grade 8

Profile: Josh works below Grade 8 level, has reading difficulties, struggles to stay on task, and may become aggressive when frustrated. He succeeds with reading assistance and oral work.

1 Access

Audio materials and reading assistance

This helps Josh understand science and social studies content without reading difficulties blocking access.

2 Response

Oral assignments and oral testing

Josh can demonstrate his knowledge without being unfairly limited by reading and writing demands.

3 Behaviour

Short tasks, planned breaks and encouragement

Clear steps, brief breaks and positive reinforcement can improve attention and reduce frustration.

✓ Main goal: Provide accessible academic work and enough structure to support regulation and success.

EA Diploma Program · Practicum 1

Guidelines, evaluation information, and the orientation and weekly task checklist.

Please review this document as well as the practicum evaluation form prior to the start of the practicum.

Roles

School Supervisor

The School Supervisor oversees and participates in the training and evaluation of the EA Practicum Student, in consultation with the EA Host. The School Supervisor can be an administrator, Learning Support Teacher, or Classroom Teacher.

EA Host

The EA Host supports the daily training of the EA Practicum student, using the evaluation document as a guide. Daily the EA Host gives direct feedback, that is clear, objective and complete, so the EA Practicum Student can grow and improve their practice. Using the Practicum 1 Evaluation Checklist as a guide, the EA Host will create time to review the progress of the EA Practicum Student, challenge them to grow in their practice, share their experience and knowledge with EA Practicum Student, and answer any questions they have.

The EA Host will also guide the EA practicum student to take direction from the classroom teacher regarding classroom procedures, adapting or modifying, and supporting students to access curriculum, while also focusing on IEP goals and objectives.

Practicum Supervisor (Burnaby Continuing Education staff)

The Practicum Supervisor will visit each school site regularly during the practicum. They will observe the EA Practicum Student, guide the reflection and goal setting process, connect with school staff, and participate in the evaluation process along with the School Supervisor and EA Host. The Practicum Supervisor will also ensure that the EA Practicum Students and school staff are informed about the intended practicum outcomes listed in the Practicum 1 Evaluation Checklist.

EA Practicum students

EA Practicum Students will review the intended practicum outcomes listed in the Practicum 1 Evaluation Checklist form prior to going on the practicum. They will work under the direction and supervision of the school team and ask for and receive feedback on practicum performance. They will inform the school and Practicum Supervisor right away in the event of illness or emergency that results in absence. They will be punctual and work the same hours as the Education Assistants in the school, any lost time will need to be made up. They will inform the Practicum Supervisor regarding questions or concerns as they arise and follow the same policies and procedures as employees in the school district.

EA Practicum students may not

  • Be out of the eyesight or earshot of a Burnaby School Board employee, either an EA or teacher, at any time, while supporting children/youth.
  • Perform intimate care duties, such as toileting or changing a child/youth.
  • Perform any routines or procedures that someone else is trained to do and signed off on, i.e., Care Plans, Safety Response Plans.
  • Use personal cell phone and/or electronic devices to take photos or record videos of children/youth. These devices are not to be visible during instructional times.
  • EA students can use their personal electronic device if directed by the EA Host for supporting students (e.g., time, looking up for a word meaning, etc.).

EA Practicum Students may

  • Observe medical support procedure.
  • Support personal care such as hand washing, feeding (not requiring nurse/OT training).
  • Support a designated child/youth providing they would normally be without an EA present, for short periods of time (less than one hour), under the direct, in class supervision of the teacher.
  • Attend meetings (e.g., EA meetings, Resource meetings, School Based Team meetings, IEP meetings, planning/collaboration meetings) as a guest observer.
  • Use personal cell phones and/or electronic devices during break times.

Evaluation

Midterm evaluation

EA Host, School Supervisor, and Practicum Supervisor

Give feedback to the practicum student that is clear, objective and complete. In advance of the midterm evaluation meeting, the School Supervisor and EA Host will complete the midterm evaluation checklist.

Final evaluation

EA Host, School Supervisor, and Practicum Supervisor

In advance of the final Evaluation meeting, the School Supervisor and EA Host will collaborate to complete the final evaluation, in ink, and present to the EA Practicum student, and the Practicum Supervisor at the final evaluation.

Please provide a copy of EA Practicum Student’s evaluation document to the EA Practicum Student at the end of evaluation session.

EA Practicum Students: Finish the 2 practicum assignments including fifteen daily reflections and hand them in to Practicum Supervisor in the last practicum debrief session.

Timeline for the practicum

We recognize that each individual school has its own culture and expectation for EA Practicum Students, and it is up to the school staff to determine if and when the EA Practicum Student is ready to move forward. The items listed below are a suggested framework to support the learning of the EA Practicum Student. If the EA Practicum Student has successfully completed the items listed below, with one child/youth in one setting, there may be opportunities for them to practice their skills across multiple settings, and multiple children/youth.

The EA Practicum Student is expected to move from observation to working directly with child/youth where appropriate and safe to do so.

School Supported Learning Experiences

With the support of the EA Host, Classroom Teacher, and School Supervisor, EA Practicum Students will begin to engage in the process of adaptation, use of visual supports (if applicable), and data collection.

EA Hosts can provide guidance and help to the EA Practicum Students for the practicum assignments. EA Practicum Students will write a reflection on their assignments and include evidence in their Learning Portfolio, to be handed in after the practicum.

  1. In collaboration with the EA Host and/or School Supervisor, create an adaptation or adaptations to assist a student with an academic task, and/or to support a social objective. This could include creating a visual support, social story, contingency map, DTT, etc. After implementing the strategy, write a reflection on how you introduced the adaptation, how it was received by the student, and add one suggestion for how it could be altered to increase its effectiveness.
  2. In collaboration with the EA Host, choose an IEP goal that is appropriate for data collection. Then, find an appropriate datasheet from your Practicum folder or one from the EA Host. Along with the EA host, take data from an identical but different data sheet on two occasions within a reasonable time. Compare the result of your data to the EA host’s data to find inconsistencies and ask for feedback. Lastly, write a reflection on the kind of datasheet that you used and discuss your findings on the student’s progress on that specific goal. In your reflections also include what strategies you would use to adjust the support program.

Practicum Learning Portfolio

The Practicum Learning Portfolio is the opportunity for EA Practicum students to reflect on their learning throughout the practicum. Reflection on one’s professional role is worthwhile not only as an EA Practicum student, but every day in the role of the Education Assistant.

Key questions are suggested below, and EA Practicum students will be expected to provide daily reflections for all 15 days of the practicum, including written reflections and copies of visuals/materials/ learning activities you have created, handed in, in their Practicum Learning Portfolio. The Practicum Learning Portfolio will be submitted to the Practicum Supervisor at the end of the practicum and is a valuable asset for the EA interview process.

Daily reflection questions

Day 1

Who were the staff that you met on your orientation day? What kind of documents and information was shared with you? How did you find that helpful?

Day 2

What were some classroom routines and norms that you have observed in the classroom? What are the types of universal supports that are available for the whole class? What are some types of essential supports for the student(s) you are supporting?

Day 3

Write a reflection on your student learning profile? What are the student’s strengths and challenges? What IEP goal do they currently have?

Day 4

What are some strategies you use to make rapport between you and the student(s) whom you are supporting? How did you approach other students? What else can you do to make the rapport bond stronger?

Day 5

What is the appropriate proximity of EA to students when supporting students? When does the EA need to be close to supporting the student(s) and when can EA allow space?

Day 6

What strategies have you observed that EA host uses and have been successful? Describe the strategies and explain why you think they were successful. Make one suggestion to improve that strategy.

Day 7

What is the routine task that you took over? What strategies did you use to support student(s) during that routine task? How can you facilitate student’s independence within that routine task?

Day 8

What is one valuable lesson you have learned so far. In which areas are you exceeding? How and in which areas can you improve yourselves for the next half of practicum 1?

Day 9

What are the direct and indirect triggers to the student(s) problem behaviours? What are the most common functions of behaviour for the student(s) you are supporting? How can you prevent the problem behaviours from happening?

Day 10

What strategies did you facilitate to promote social inclusion between the student whom you are supporting and other students in instructional and non-instructional times? Make one or two suggestions to improve your strategies.

Day 11

Explain how you are facilitating student’s independence during instructional and non-instructional times. Provide some examples.

Day 12

Explain the consequence strategies you are implementing and evaluate their effectiveness. How can you improve them?

Day 13

During your first practicum experience, explain a time that there was a conflict between you and the EA host and/or classroom teacher or explain a time that you witnessed a conflict. What was the conflict about and how it was resolved? What did you learn?

Day 14

How has your understanding of the role of Education Assistant changed since you began this practicum? Explain one or two strategies that you’ve observed so far that seemed to work with the student(s) that you would add to your “toolkit”.

Day 15

(a) What were your successes? What was satisfying? What will you keep doing on your next practicum? (b) What remains challenging? What would you like to learn more about for your next practicum?

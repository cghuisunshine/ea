Practicum 1 – Self-Evaluation Checklist

The Assessment Checklist is intended to guide the EA Students in providing ongoing self-evaluation and self-reflection throughout the practicum. Three weekly self-evaluations and self-reflections are to be completed by the EA student in advance to follow up with practicum supervisor. Each self-evaluation should be completed by the end of each week of Practicum, to provide students with the opportunity to adjust their ongoing performance for the remainder of their practicum experience. The Practicum Supervisor and EA host will check each week’s entry and provide feedback on EA students’ Self-evaluation and will provide suggestions for improvement.

For each criterion, select a rating for Week 1, Week 2, and Week 3.

Accountability and Safety

  1. Arrives prepared and on time.
  2. Uses personal electronic devices only during designated breaks times.*
  3. Able to explain the legal responsibility to report incidents of child abuse or neglect.
  4. Uses feedback to enhance skills and abilities.
  5. Behaves in a professional manner at all times.

Professional Attire

  1. Wears comfortable, professional clothing that allows for physical activity, with full coverage, and without questionable logos or references, rips, or tears.
  2. Wears supportive footwear with non-slip soles, securely attached to the foot.
  3. Secures long hair and removes loose items, such as long jewelry.

Ethics and Respect

  1. Maintains professional boundaries with school staff, students, and families.
  2. Protects confidential student, staff, and parent information, verbally and in writing.
  3. Discusses students in classroom, hallway and staffroom with a strong focus on protecting the students’ privacy and dignity at all times.
  4. Accepts and supports the decisions and directions of school staff.
  5. Demonstrates a strong awareness and practice of respectful language related to race, culture, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, and disability.

Communication and Collaboration

  1. Models respectful and effective verbal and non-verbal communication.
  2. Respects other’s ideas and perspectives.
  3. Communicates student progress to the classroom teacher, verbally and in writing.
  4. Reviews all written information, as available, and asks questions to gather more information helpful to the support of the students.
  5. Demonstrates an understanding that home/school communications are to be conducted by the classroom teacher and directs parent inquiries to the teacher.
  6. Demonstrates a strong ability to communicate effectively in English.
  7. Engages with students and school staff in a positive and cheerful manner.

Administrative Tasks / Non-Instructional Support

  1. Knows where all student documentation is located. (e.g. IEP, SRP, PBS Plan)
  2. With support, records and tracks data as outlined in student IEPs.
  3. Able to complete timecards and absence logging as necessary.
  4. Understands roles and responsibilities of school and district staff.

Student Support

  1. Demonstrates an understanding of inclusion, and the ability to facilitate it.
  2. Takes initiative to support student needs, while taking direction from the classroom teacher and EA Host.
  3. Demonstrates an ability to establish themselves as an authority figure in the class.
  4. Provides specific, positive feedback to students, with a focus on competencies, to build confidence and self esteem.
  5. Provides clear and consistent direction and follows through.
  6. With assistance, uses supports to support inclusion and self regulation. (visual schedule, timers, transition countdown, contingency maps, social stories)
  7. With assistance, reinforces socially appropriate behaviour.
  8. With assistance, understands and implements break and reward times.
  9. With assistance, collects and reviews data to assess social and behavioural needs.
  10. With assistance, seeks to understand the function of student behaviour.
  11. With assistance, encourages student engagement amongst peers during instructional and non-instructional time
  12. With assistance, facilitates opportunities to develop friendships by creating or developing connections amongst students.
  13. Understands the impact of different experiences and cultural backgrounds.

Facilitating Independence

  1. With assistance, uses developmentally and age appropriate adaptations and modifications to support student inclusion in the classroom
  2. With assistance, uses the lowest level of prompting required, as outlined in the Prompting Hierarchy
  3. With assistance, identifies strategies that may be used to reduce student reliance on EA support
  4. With assistance, provides opportunities for students to practice self-monitoring and problem solving
  5. Honours student perspective and input

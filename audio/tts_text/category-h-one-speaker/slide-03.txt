Start with what he can do.

Liam is a good writer. Writing shows what he knows better than speaking does.

He plays guitar and writes his own songs.

He works well with a small, steady team of adults he trusts.

He likes a quiet work space, the same adults, and the same routine.

He likes short lists. Two or three jobs, not a long one.

Play means his own time. He records songs at home, and music through headphones helps him settle.

He likes one or two friends, not a big group. He does not enjoy loud events like dances or assemblies.

So invite him to the small things, not the loud ones.

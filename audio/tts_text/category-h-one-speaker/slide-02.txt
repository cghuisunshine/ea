Category H is for students who need the most support at school.

It is used when a serious mental illness, or behaviour that keeps happening, makes learning very hard.

The category is about the effect on learning. It is not about how smart the student is.

Liam has bipolar I disorder. A psychiatrist made the diagnosis.

His mood, his sleep and his energy change a lot. Some weeks he is very low. Some weeks he is very high.

This affects his attendance, his focus and the choices he makes.

The school, his family and his doctors work from one plan.

We ask three questions. What can he already do? What is getting in the way? What support will help?

One more thing. A profile is short — one or two pages. It is for staff who help him now and then. It does not replace the IEP or the safety plan.

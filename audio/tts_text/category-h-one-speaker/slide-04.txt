Communication has two sides.

Receptive is how he takes information in.

When he is well, he understands Grade 10 language easily. When he is tired or unwell, he understands less.

So use short, calm sentences. One instruction at a time. Write it down as well as saying it.

Give him time to answer.

No jokes or sarcasm when he is upset. He can take them the wrong way.

Expressive is how he tells us what he needs.

He talks and he writes. Writing is usually clearer for him.

He uses a scale from 1 to 5. One means he needs to leave now. Five means he is doing well.

He uses a break card. When it goes on his desk, say yes straight away.

When he is low he says very little. When he is high he talks fast.

Speak to Liam, not about him. Do not speak for him.

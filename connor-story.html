I’m proud of how far I’ve come.

A story about finding my voice, learning new skills, helping my family, and never giving up.

Read my story ↓

Step by step

Hard work changes what is possible.

01

I work hard

I’m proud of myself because I work very hard. I try my best at everything because, for me, almost everything is hard. I know that I have to work super hard to accomplish my goals.

02

Finding my voice

When I came to school in kindergarten, I couldn’t talk. I couldn’t talk in grade one either. In grades two and three, I worked very hard but still couldn’t talk. I didn’t give up. Ms. Stephens kept encouraging me and teaching me words. In grade four, I started talking.

03

Learning to read and write

Once I started talking, I started to learn to read. Ms. Stephens helped me every day, and I worked hard every day. I practised my letter sounds, word sounds, and vocabulary. It only took a year, and now I can read.

While I was learning how to read, Ms. Stephens taught me how to write. It was hard, and I know that I’m still learning. My writing needs to be neater, and my spelling sucks, but that doesn’t stop me. It makes me work harder because every day I am determined to get better.

04

Being a big brother

I am a big brother. I need to be able to help my family, and that’s the most important thing. I’m proud of how far I’ve come, but I know I still have far to go.

05

Helping at home

I help my grandma take the garbage out. I pick up the dog poop, wash and dry the dishes, clean out the cat’s litter box and put it in the garbage bag, and take the recycling out. I also help with my little puppy and take care of the puppy.

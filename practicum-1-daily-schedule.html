Practicum 1 · Suggested action plan

What to do each day

This 15-day plan combines the weekly guidelines, the two IEP-related assignments, daily reflection prompts, and the evaluation forms. The day-by-day placement is a suggested way to pace the work; follow your EA Host and school team when they adjust it.

Before you start and every day

Another EA student has already sent the group introduction and copied your EA Host, so there is no need to repeat that email.

Read the guidelines and evaluation criteria. Arrive on time, sign in as directed, follow the school team's instructions, ask for feedback, and protect student privacy. Stay within the supervision and task limits in the guidelines.

Write one daily reflection for each of the 15 days and keep it in your learning portfolio. The source leaves the assignment due date blank; confirm your meeting and submission dates with your Practicum Supervisor. Checkmarks and dates on this page save in this browser.

Week 1 · Observe and learn

Days 1–5

Focus on orientation, safety, student information, relationships, and a consistent routine. Move from observing to direct support only when your school team considers it appropriate.

Day 1 · Orientation

Reflection: Who did you meet, what information was shared, and how was it helpful? Full Day 1 prompt

Day 2 · Classroom routines

Reflection: What routines and universal or essential supports did you see? Full Day 2 prompt

Day 3 · Student profile and IEP

Reflection: What are the student’s strengths, challenges, and IEP goal? Full Day 3 prompt

Day 4 · Rapport and communication

Reflection: How did you build rapport, and what could strengthen it? Full Day 4 prompt

Day 5 · Proximity and Week 1 review

Reflection: When should you be near a student, and when can you allow space? Full Day 5 prompt

Week 1 milestone: Complete Week 1 self-evaluation and reflection. Keep the Week 1 guidelines checklist up to date.

Week 2 · Practise with support

Days 6–10

With your Host, begin IEP support and data collection. Observe behaviour, promote independence and social inclusion, and keep building relationships.

Day 6 · Effective strategies

Reflection: Which Host strategy worked, why, and what might improve it? Full Day 6 prompt

Day 7 · Routine and independence

Reflection: What routine did you take over, and how did you support independence? Full Day 7 prompt

Day 8 · Check progress

Reflection: What have you learned, where are you doing well, and what will you improve? Full Day 8 prompt

Day 9 · Behaviour and data

Reflection: What triggers or functions of behaviour did you notice, and how might you prevent problems? Full Day 9 prompt

Day 10 · Inclusion and Week 2 review

Reflection: How did you promote social inclusion, and what would you improve? Full Day 10 prompt

Week 2 milestone: Complete Week 2 self-evaluation and reflection. The School Supervisor and EA Host prepare the midterm evaluation before the scheduled meeting; review their feedback rather than filling in their ratings yourself.

Week 3 · Apply and finish

Days 11–15

Take a fuller EA role while still supported by your Host. Finish assignment evidence and reflections, then prepare for the final evaluation and debrief.

Day 11 · More independent support

Reflection: How did you help the student act independently in different settings? Full Day 11 prompt

Day 12 · Evaluate strategies

Reflection: How effective were the consequence strategies, and what could improve? Full Day 12 prompt

Day 13 · Practise and resolve

Reflection: Describe a conflict you experienced or observed, how it was resolved, and what you learned. Full Day 13 prompt

Day 14 · Prepare to submit

Reflection: How has your view of the EA role changed, and which strategies will you keep? Full Day 14 prompt

Day 15 · Finish and debrief

Reflection: What succeeded, what remains challenging, and what do you want to learn next? Full Day 15 prompt

Final milestone: Complete Week 3 self-evaluation, both IEP-based assignment records, and your 15-day learning portfolio. The school staff prepare the final evaluation before the scheduled meeting.
